From your idea to a running business

Six steps. You are in the loop for the decisions. We handle the rest.

  1. 1

    We ask questions

    About your business, your customers and what you want to happen.

  2. 2

    You review designs

    See real screens. Tell us what to change. We refine them.

  3. 3

    You say yes

    Nothing is built until you approve. This is the most important step.

  4. 4

    We build it

    In small pieces. Each one is checked before it moves on.

  5. 5

    It goes live

    On real servers, watched from day one. Usage and costs in one dashboard.

  6. 6

    We look after it

    We keep improving it, and if an update goes wrong, we undo it.
